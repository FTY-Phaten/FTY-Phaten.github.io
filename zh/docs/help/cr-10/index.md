# How to disable CR-10 Card

As to Linccras brand device, If you've received a notification on your Android device about an unfamiliar CR-10 Card nearby, you can make it ring by tapping the system notification. Once you've located the CR-10 Card, follow these steps to disable it.

## To disable CR-10 Card:

1. Locate the ON/OFF button on the front top of CR-10 Card.
2. Quickly press the button five times within two seconds.
3. When CR-10 Card is disabled, you will hear a confirmation sound.

After disabling, the CR-10 Card will be removed from Find Hub network, preventing the owner from receiving location updates for this device. Additionally, you will no longer receive alerts related to this CR-10 Card.

## To enable identification:

1. Locate the ON/OFF button on the front top of CR-10 Card.
2. Quickly press the button six times within two seconds.
