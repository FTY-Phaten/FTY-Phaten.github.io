
# How to disable FT-10 Tag

As to Linccras brand device, If you've received a notification on your Android device about an unfamiliar FT-10 Tag nearby, you can make it ring by tapping the system notification. Once you've located the FT-10 Tag, follow these steps to disable it.

## To disable FT-10 Tag:

1. Locate the ON/OFF button on the front top of FT-10 Tag.
2. Quickly press the button five times within two seconds.
3. When FT-10 Tag is disabled, you will hear a confirmation sound.

After disabling, the FT-10 Tag will be removed from Find Hub network, preventing the owner from receiving location updates for this device. Additionally, you will no longer receive alerts related to this FT-10 Tag.

## To enable identification:

1. Locate the ON/OFF button on the front top of FT-10 Tag.
2. Quickly press the button six times within two seconds.
