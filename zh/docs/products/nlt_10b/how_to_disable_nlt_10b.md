# How to Disable an NLT-10B Tag

## Overview

The NLT-10B Tag can be disabled to prevent location sharing. Once disabled, the device will no longer report to Google's Find My Device network, and the owner will not receive location updates.

If you receive a notification on your Android device indicating that an NLT-10B Tag has been detected nearby and you do not recognize this device, you can trigger the tag to ring through the system notification. After locating the device, follow the steps below to disable it.

Once disabled, the owner will no longer receive location updates and the tag will not trigger unknown tracker alerts.

---

## Disabling the NLT-10B Tag

### Method: Press and Hold Sequence

1. Locate the button on the front of the NLT-10B Tag.

2. Quickly press the button **ten times**.

3. Press and hold the button for **five seconds**.

4. Two confirmation beeps will sound, indicating the tag is disabled.

!!! success "Confirmation"
    Two beeps confirm successful disabling of the tag.

---

## Re-enabling the NLT-10B Tag

To re-enable the tag:

1. Press the button **once** quickly.

2. A "ding ling ling" sound will play, confirming the tag is re-enabled.

!!! info "Audio Confirmation"
    The distinctive "ding ling ling" tone indicates successful re-activation.

---

## Troubleshooting

### Operational Notes

- Ensure you complete the full sequence (10 quick presses + 5-second hold) to disable
- Interrupting the sequence will require restarting from the beginning
- Audio feedback is essential for confirming state changes

---

## Safety and Privacy Information

### When to Disable

- Upon detecting an unfamiliar NLT-10B Tag in your vicinity
- To temporarily suspend location tracking
- To conserve battery power during extended non-use periods

### Privacy Protection

Disabling unknown tracking devices protects your privacy and prevents unauthorized location monitoring. Always verify the ownership of any tracking device found in your possession.
